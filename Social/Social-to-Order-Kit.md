## 1. Facebook "Add a button" (steps for Shane)

Shane does this on his phone or computer while logged in as himself (he has to be an admin of the Page).

**On a computer (easiest):**
1. Go to facebook.com and **switch into the Burnett's Butcher Shop Page**. Click your profile picture (top right), then click **Burnett's Butcher Shop**.
2. Under the cover photo, click **+ Add button**. If there's already a button like "Message" or "Call", click the **pencil / Edit** next to it.
3. Pick an order-style button. Facebook changes these labels, so use the first one you see from this list:
   **Order Food** → **Shop Now** → **Learn More**
4. When it asks where the button goes, choose **Website** / **Link to website**, then paste in:
   `https://burnettsorders.bwabarrie.ca`
5. Click **Save**.
6. **Test it:** log out (or open the Page on your phone from a friend's account), tap the button, and make sure the order form opens.

**On the phone (Facebook app):**
1. Tap the menu (☰), then tap your name at the top and switch to **Burnett's Butcher Shop**.
2. On the Page, tap **Edit** (or the **···** under the cover photo), then **Add action button** / **Edit action button**.
3. Same as above: pick **Order Food** (or **Shop Now** / **Learn More**), choose **Website**, paste the link, **Save**.

**Also worth 30 seconds:** Page **Settings → Page info / Contact info → Website** should be `https://burnettsorders.bwabarrie.ca` until his real site is back. Once burnettsbutchershop.com is recovered and has its own "Order Online" button, switch the Website field back to that.
